Seal Survivor
You are a seal. You want to eat all the fish. Sharks are your competition — take them out before they take your lunch.
Your uneaten chum bits float to the sea floor. Swim down to collect them for XP and health.
Watch out for crabs! They gather in large numbers to scavenge your leftovers, and they will pinch ya.
And don't forget to breathe. Realistic mammal needs are in full effect.

Desktop: WASD to steer, mouse to aim, hold click to fire. Space to strike.
Mobile: drag to aim and fire.  ·  Gamepad: sticks to move/aim, RT or A to fire, B to strike.
