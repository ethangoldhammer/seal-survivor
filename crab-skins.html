Crab shells — pattern options

Every pattern in the generator, on both crabs, through the real asset pipeline and the game's own bloom. The DAY crab (carapace) is pigment: flow, breath and flicker are all zero, so whatever is on its shell never moves. The NIGHT crab (emberClaw) is light, and its pattern drifts and breathes. Filmstrips are how motion is shown — this page renders one frame per tile and has no animation loop. Everything here is on sliders: ` → Look & FX → Bioluminescence.
