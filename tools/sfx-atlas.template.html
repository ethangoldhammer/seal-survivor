The Sound Atlas

Every sound the game can make, what fires it, and where from — joined up from CONFIG.sfx, CONFIG.feedback, the call sites in gameplay code, the files in public/sfx, and the tuning file that quietly overrides all of it. Press play on any row to hear it: the sampled sounds are embedded, and the synthesised ones are rebuilt here by the game's own synth, through the game's own filter and reverb bus.

Signal flow

Four kinds of sound, two chains out

One-shots, the ambient bed and the projectile flight voices all land on the shared SFX bus, so diving muffles them together. Music deliberately does not — it has its own filter, its own depth range, and its own duck for the upgrade screen.

The bank

Every voice in CONFIG.sfx

A sound with files assigned plays those and ignores its synth settings entirely — the synth block stays behind as the fallback if a file ever fails to decode, which is why it is still worth reading. Open a row for the full recipe, the events that fire it, and the lines of code they fire from. For the same information live, press 0 in a dev build: the sound feed names each sound as it fires, which take of the set it picked, and — the part this page cannot show you — the ones that were throttled, refused a voice, or never existed.
